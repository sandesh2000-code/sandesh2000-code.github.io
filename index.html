Solar Architecture Profile

Solar Panels

Made of silicon cells to catch sunlight. Monocrystalline types give higher power output.

Inverter

Changes direct current (DC) power from panels into alternating current (AC) for home use.

Mounting Racks

Holds the panels at the right structural angle on your roof layout.

Net Meter

Tracks power sent to and taken from the utility grid system.

Eco-Friendly

Produces clean, green energy without generating harmful air pollution metrics.

Roof Protection

Shades your physical roof structure from direct sun, which can help keep your top floor spaces cooler.
